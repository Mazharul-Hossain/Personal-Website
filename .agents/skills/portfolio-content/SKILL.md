# Portfolio content maintenance

Read the [content map](../../../docs/README.md#content-ownership-and-migration), [README contracts](../../../README.md#behavioral-contracts), and [active-plan MIG-01/MIG-06](../../../docs/BUNDLE_OPTIMIZATION_PLAN.md) before moving facts. Code paths are repository-root-relative.

## Evidence and disclosure

Use approved facts and available primary evidence. Separate supported facts from permission to publish. Do not infer duties, dates, awards, metrics, figure rights or affiliations. Preserve LinkedIn-first contact and the choice not to publish a CV download/private contact details. Keep unknowns explicit with a concrete question.

The editorial handoff is a source-checking guide, not proof its old draft remains current. Historical proposals do not determine selection count or layout. Inspect current records/tests rather than restoring historical selections.

## One authority, rendered views

Reusable structured facts belong in TypeScript. HTML renders records with bindings/control flow; narrative-only presentation and composition may remain in templates. Do not copy identical citations into another independent store.

For MIG-01, inventory complete legacy bibliography HTML: authors/order, titles, venues, years/status, URLs/DOIs, theses, categories/order. Use stable publication IDs and an ordered selected-ID list. Research links resolve shared IDs/URLs. Keep full details in the lazy bibliography; share only the core needed eagerly. Measure initial/lazy chunks after changing imports.

Until migration lands, check corrections against selected TS records, full HTML citations and research records. Preserve information and register mismatches. An active plan is not an implemented shared model.

## Validate

Check affected views, authors, selection/category order, captions/alt text, metadata and external links. Distinguish publication from preprint/submission. Preserve attribution/copyright disclosure. Asset replacement requires readable diagrams and verified reuse rights; public download does not establish permission.

Add `TODO<Content>[ID]` and an active-register entry for deferred evidence work. Missing-fact markers are not permanent test fixtures. Report sources used, changed facts and unresolved questions; do not claim unavailable CVs/sources were inspected.
