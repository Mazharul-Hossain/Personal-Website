About

Research grounded in measurements and real-world change

I am an AI researcher working at the intersection of computational imaging, scientific machine learning, and robust computer vision. My research asks how learning systems can produce dependable results when measurements are incomplete and deployment conditions differ from training.

My work has progressed from physics-aware microscopy reconstruction to generalizable hyperspectral analysis and biomedical computational imaging. Across these settings, I combine knowledge of the sensing process with data-driven models, aiming for methods that are scientifically grounded and useful beyond a single benchmark.
